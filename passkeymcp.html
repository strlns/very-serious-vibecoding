FAQ

Before you make yourself optional.

Do I need a specific password manager?

Connect the identity provider your workflow already uses. Your agents receive a consistent interface, without needing to agree on where a credential belongs.

What if I lose my phone?

Continue from another device or a running agent session. Your identity no longer depends on the availability of a particular piece of hardware.

Can I switch AI providers?

Yes. Carry your credential context into the next agent, along with the permissions it needs to continue. No new introductions. No uncomfortable questions about the previous model.

What happens to my TOTP codes?

They continue to rotate every thirty seconds. Our compatibility layer continues to ignore them. Existing authenticator apps do not need to be uninstalled.

How long does an approval last?

For as long as the work remains relevant. Relevance is evaluated continuously by the agent carrying out the work.

Who approves an agent’s request for more access?

Your designated identity agent. If the requesting agent already holds that role, the approval stays within the same context. This reduces coordination overhead.

What happens when I revoke access?

Revocation is added to the shared context so your agents can take it into account when planning subsequent access requests.

Can an agent confirm that I’m present?

Yes. It has access to your identity, your recent activity, and a working model of your preferences. In most workflows, that is more context than a person pressing a button provides.

Can I still sign in myself?

Of course. Human access is fully supported. You may be asked to confirm with your primary agent.

Do you actually want this?

No, I don’t actually want this. This is a fictional product; the demo runs locally and does not connect to any vault, account, or service.
